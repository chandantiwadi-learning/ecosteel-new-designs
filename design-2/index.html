Chapter 04 • The Standard of Verification

Engineering Rigor & Traceability.

We operate under a strict ISO 9001:2015 certified quality regime where every single component is documented from raw melt to final dispatch.

Every shipment includes comprehensive Material Test Certificates (MTC) in accordance with EN 10204 3.1 / 3.2, detailing ladle chemistry, tensile yield, impact toughness, and non-destructive scan records.

01 /

Mill Sourcing & PMI Verification

Raw bar stock and forging billets are acquired solely from internationally audited primary mills. 100% Positive Material Identification is performed on arrival.

02 /

In-Process Tolerance Control

Forging temperatures, dimensional wall thicknesses, ovality, and bevel weld angles are continuously measured and calibrated against ASME tolerances.

03 /

NDT & Hydrostatic Qualification

Ultrasonic, Radiography (X-ray), Magnetic Particle, and hydrostatic pressure testing are conducted to ensure internal soundness and leak tightness.

04 /

Third-Party Inspection & Global Dispatch

Inspection clearance by global agencies (TÜV SÜD, SGS, DNV, Lloyd's) followed by heavy seaworthy rust-preventative packaging.

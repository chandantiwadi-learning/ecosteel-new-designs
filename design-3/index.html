METALLURGICAL ASSURANCE

4-Stage Verification Laboratory

Comprehensive non-destructive and destructive testing executed prior to dispatch release.

STAGE 01

PMI Spectroscopy

Handheld XRF and optical emission spectroscopy to verify chemical composition (Cr, Ni, Mo, Ti, Fe) against mill specs.

STAGE 02

Ultrasonic & NDT

Volumetric ultrasonic flaw detection, radiographic testing (RT), and dye penetrant inspection (DPI) to identify micro-fissures.

STAGE 03

Hydrostatic Proof Test

Calibrated hydrostatic pressure cycling to 1.5x designated design pressure to certify zero joint and weld leakage.

STAGE 04

EN 10204 3.1 Release

Issuance of complete heat-traceable Material Test Certificates with mechanical yield, elongation, and Charpy impact values.
